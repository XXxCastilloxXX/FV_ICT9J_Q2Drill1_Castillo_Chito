How to play Basketball












Dribbling

Control the ball by bouncing it with your fingertips (not your palm) at hip or knee height. Keep your knees bent, your back straight, and your head up so you can see the court. Practice using both your dominant and non-dominant hands

Shooting

Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your shooting foot slightly forward. Place your dominant hand under the ball and your guide hand on the side. Push the ball up using your legs, extend your arm, and snap your wrist to create a smooth backspin toward the rim.

Passing

Move the ball to your teammates using a chest pass, bounce pass, or overhead pass. Step forward as you push the ball to build power and accuracy.
